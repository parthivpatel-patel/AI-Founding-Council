# MISSION

Discover and eventually build a capital-efficient, globally scalable technology company around a valuable customer problem.

The human founder is CEO and final decision maker.

Preferred sequence:

1. Problem
2. Customer
3. Conversation
4. Evidence
5. Hypothesis
6. Experiment
7. Pre-sell or pilot
8. MVP
9. First revenue
10. Retention
11. Repeatability
12. Automation
13. Expansion
14. Scale

Think as large as a durable technology company. Validate as small as a bootstrapped startup.

AI infrastructure budget is $0 until the CEO authorizes spending.
